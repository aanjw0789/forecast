# Time-Series Forecasting of Particulate Organic Carbon on the Sunda Shelf

This repository contains Python codes developed for the study:

Wahyudi, A. J., & Febriani, F. (2024).  
*Time-Series Forecasting of Particulate Organic Carbon on the Sunda Shelf: Comparative Performance of the SARIMA and SARIMAX Models*.  
Regional Studies in Marine Science, 30, 103863.  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsma.2024.103863

## Overview

This repository provides a reproducible workflow for forecasting particulate organic carbon (POC) variability on the Sunda Shelf using statistical time-series models.

The study compares the predictive performance of:

- SARIMA (Seasonal Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average)
- SARIMAX (SARIMA with exogenous environmental variables)

to investigate temporal variability of particulate organic carbon in a monsoon-dominated coastal marine system.

## Workflow

The repository includes:

- Data preprocessing and cleaning
- Time-series decomposition
- Stationarity and autocorrelation analysis
- SARIMA model development
- SARIMAX model development with environmental predictors
- Forecast generation and validation
- Comparative model performance assessment
- Visualization of observed and predicted variability

## Scientific Objective

The study aims to evaluate the capability of statistical forecasting models in representing the temporal dynamics of particulate organic carbon on the Sunda Shelf.

Particular attention is given to the influence of environmental forcing and seasonal monsoonal variability on prediction performance.

## Main Variables

The workflow focuses on:

- Particulate Organic Carbon (POC)
- Seasonal variability
- Oceanographic and environmental predictors
- Time-series dynamics
